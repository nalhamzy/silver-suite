## Full description

> **Big buttons. Clear text. Real help.**
>
> Silver Suite is the senior-friendly utility kit that actually feels like a premium app — not a patronizing clip-art nightmare. One calm, high-contrast home screen, six tools older users actually open every day.
>
> **Tools included**
>
> ★ Big clock
> ★ Pill reminders — color-coded, multiple times per day, one-tap "Taken"
> ★ One-tap calling — mark any contact as Emergency to pin at top
> ★ SOS screen — pulsing 911 button with a confirm step + emergency shortcuts
> ★ Flashlight — torch + screen-light fallback
> ★ Magnifier — 24-120pt text scale, high-contrast mode, bold toggle
> ★ Big-text notes
> ★ Simple calculator — four-function, oversized keys
>
> **Designed for 60+ eyes and shaky taps**
> · Tap targets start at 56dp
> · Dark mode for night use
> · Text-size presets: Standard / Large / Extra Large
> · No tiny icons. No buried menus.
>
> **Silver+ (optional)**
> Removes the banner ad and unlocks unlimited medications, contacts, and notes.
> $29.99 launch lifetime. One payment, no subscription. Removes ads and unlocks unlimited medications, contacts, and notes.
>
> **Privacy first.** No account. Everything on-device. AdMob for the banner on the free tier — nothing else. No data brokers.
