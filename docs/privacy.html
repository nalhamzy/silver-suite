Privacy Policy

Effective date: 17 April 2026

TL;DR — No account. No analytics. Your data stays on your phone. The free tier shows a banner ad via Google AdMob — upgrade removes it permanently.

What Silver Suite itself collects

Nothing. There is no analytics pipeline, no crash reporter, no server we control. The app has no account system.

Ads (free tier only, via Google AdMob)

When ads are enabled — meaning you haven't yet bought Remove Ads or any Silver+ plan — the Google Mobile Ads SDK runs inside the app. By default we request non-personalized ads. AdMob receives:

  • Your device's advertising identifier (IDFA on iOS, AAID on Android)
  • Ad request metadata (country-level IP, ad unit ID, app version)

The moment you buy Remove Ads or Silver+, the AdMob SDK is no longer called. No further ad requests are made from your device.

AdMob policy: policies.google.com/privacy

What lives on your device

  • Pill schedules + taken-today log
  • Contacts (name, phone, relation, emergency flag)
  • Notes
  • Display settings (dark mode, text-size preset)
  • Premium state (which tier you bought)

Nothing here leaves the device. There is no cloud sync. Uninstalling the app deletes all of it.

In-App Purchases

Processed entirely by Apple App Store / Google Play Billing. Silver Suite receives only a signed receipt with the product ID + purchase state, used to flip local flags. We never see your card, Apple ID, or Google account email.

Permissions

PermissionWhy
CameraTo toggle the torch LED (required by torch_light). We never capture photos or video.
InternetAdMob ad requests on the free tier only.
BillingTo talk to Play Billing.

No contacts, calendar, microphone, or location permissions are requested — ever.

Phone calls

When you tap a contact's call button, Silver Suite invokes the system dialer via a standard tel: URL. We do not place the call, intercept it, or log it.

GDPR / California / Australia rights

  • Access — everything is on your device.
  • Deletion — uninstall the app.
  • Opt out of sale — no sale happens.
  • Automated decision-making — none occurs.

EU/UK users: legal basis for processing the advertising identifier (free tier only) is consent. Withdraw by upgrading to any paid tier, or via device-level settings.

Children

Silver Suite is designed for adults. It is not directed at children under 13 and we do not knowingly collect personal information from them.
